# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Lately saves the Instagram reels you share to it, turns each one into a card you can search, and keeps your reasons for saving it. This policy explains what Lately stores, who helps process it, and how to delete it.

## What Lately stores

- **Your account.** Your email address. If you sign in with Google, also the name and profile photo Google shares with us. Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth.
- **Your saves.** The links to the reels you share or paste into Lately, and when you saved them.
- **Your notes.** The "why are you saving this?" notes you write, and any edits to them.
- **Cards Lately makes from your saves.** For each saved reel, Lately reads its public content (caption, thumbnail, and the audio, which is transcribed to text) and derives a card from it: a title, a summary, steps, places, tags and a category. To make search work, Lately also computes search data (text indexes and numeric "embeddings") from your cards and notes.
- **Your stacks and settings.** The collections you create and which saves are in them, and your reading preferences (font, text size, theme).
- **Usage limits.** How many reels you have saved this month, so the free plan's monthly limit can be applied.

On your phone, Lately also keeps a few things locally: your sign-in session, links you shared while offline (until they are sent), a shared link waiting for you to sign in, and your recent searches.

## What Lately does not do

- Lately does not sell your data or share it with advertisers.
- Lately does not show ads and does not include advertising or analytics trackers.
- Lately does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, files, contacts or location. It only receives the text of what you share to it.
- Lately does not post anything to Instagram or to any other account of yours.

## Who helps process your data

Lately uses these service providers to run. Each receives only what it needs for its task:

- **Supabase** — sign-in, the database that stores your account, saves, notes, cards and stacks, and file storage for thumbnails.
- **OpenRouter and the language-model provider it routes to** — receives a reel's public caption, transcript and thumbnail to write the card (title, summary, steps, places, tags, category).
- **Groq** — receives a reel's audio to transcribe it to text.
- **OpenAI** — receives the text of your cards and notes to compute search embeddings, and the text of your searches to match them.
- **Our hosting provider** — runs the Lately server that connects these together.

Your searches are processed to return results; they are not used for advertising.

## How long data is kept

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete a save in the app, its link, note, card and search data are deleted. When you delete your account, everything listed above is deleted. Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a limited period before they are overwritten.

## Your choices and rights

- **See and edit.** Everything Lately stores about your saves is visible in the app, and notes can be edited at any time.
- **Export.** In Account and Settings, "Download Vault Archive" sends you a copy of your reels and notes.
- **Delete a save.** Open a card, tap the ⋮ menu, then Delete.
- **Delete your account.** In Account and Settings, tap "Delete account" and confirm twice. This deletes your account and everything in it, then signs you out.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights (such as access, correction or objection). You can exercise them through the app or by contacting us.

## Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each account can only read its own saves, notes, cards and stacks.

## Children

Lately is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes will be pointed out in the app.

## Contact

Questions or requests about your data: write to the support email shown on Lately's Google Play listing.
