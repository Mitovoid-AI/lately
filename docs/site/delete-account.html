Delete your Lately account

This page is for Lately, the Android app listed on Google Play as “Lately: Find Saved Reels”. You can delete your account and everything in it from the app, or by email without the app.

Subscribed to Lately Pro? Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription, and neither does uninstalling the app. Cancel it first in Google Play subscriptions (or Manage subscription in the app), signed in to the Google account you subscribed with.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Lately and tap your profile picture or initial at the top of the Home screen. This opens Account and settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Continue, then Delete forever.

Your account and its data are deleted right away, and you are signed out. Want a copy first? Download your archive (a JSON file of your saves, notes, cards and stacks) in Account and settings before you delete.

Delete it without the app

Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the address you sign in to Lately with (for Google sign-in, your Google account's address), with the subject “Delete my account”. We only act on requests sent from the account's own address. We delete the account and its data within [DAYS] days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your email address, and the name and profile photo Google shared if you signed in with Google.
  • Your saves (the reel links), your notes, and the search data made from them.
  • Your stacks, reading settings, monthly save count and plan details, including the subscription updates Lately received from RevenueCat.
  • Your latest data archive and any work still queued for your saves.

What is kept

  • Cards made from a reel's public content are shared by everyone who saved that reel and hold none of your data, so they stay.
  • Google keeps its own record of any Google Play purchase, under Google's privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat keeps its purchase record under your account ID (a random identifier, not your name or email) until we delete it. Ask for this in your email, or email us after deleting in the app, and we delete it too.
  • Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a limited period before they are overwritten.

Delete some data, keep the account

  • One save: open its card, tap the ⋮ menu, then Delete. Its link, your note and your copy of the save are deleted.
  • A note: open the card and edit the note, or clear it.
  • A stack: open the stack and delete it. The saves in it stay in your library.

The privacy policy has the full list of what Lately stores and how long it is kept.
