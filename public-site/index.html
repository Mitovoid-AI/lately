Lately

Save the Instagram reels you want to come back to, and find them later by typing what you remember.

  • Share a reel to Lately from Instagram, or paste its link. Add a note on why you saved it if you like.
  • Lately turns the reel into a card: a title, a summary, steps, places and tags.
  • Search your saves by a word, a place or what the reel was about. Group them into stacks.

Lately is an Android app on Google Play.
